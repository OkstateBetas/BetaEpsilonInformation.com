Qualifications

We seek individuals who:

  • Are passionate about Sigma Lambda Beta's history, mission, and values.
  • Are enrolled at a college or university where there is an active SLB collegiate entity.

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum 2.50 cumulative GPA at the time of application.
  • First-semester students must provide proof of a 3.0 unweighted high school GPA (4.0 scale).

Participation Requirements

  • Attend one MGC Informational meeting.
  • Attend one Beta Informational meeting.

If you are unable to attend any of the scheduled MGC or Beta Informationals, please contact us through the Contact Us page to discuss alternative arrangements.

Membership is only available to collegiate men who have received an official bid by a collegiate entity of Sigma Lambda Beta.

*For questions regarding qualifications, please reach out through the Contact Us page.
